Terms & Community Guidelines

Last updated September 2026 · Applies to every Extroverts account

1. Who this is for

Extroverts is a space for people 18 and older to organize and join real, in-person gatherings. By continuing, you confirm the information you provide during signup — including your date of birth — is accurate.

2. Your account

You're responsible for keeping your login details private and for anything that happens under your account. One person, one account — we don't allow accounts created on someone else's behalf.

3. How we verify you

  • We'll send a one-time code to confirm the email you sign up with.
  • We ask for your age to keep the community 18+, and your general location so nearby parties make sense.
  • You can request your data be deleted at any time from account settings.

4. Showing up

RSVPing to a gathering is a real commitment to real people who blocked out real time. Repeated no-shows or unsafe behavior at events can result in suspension.

5. What we don't allow

  • Harassment, hate speech, or discrimination toward other members
  • Using the app to sell, solicit, or promote unrelated services
  • Misrepresenting your identity, age, or the nature of an event

6. Changes

We'll let you know if these terms change in any way that affects your rights. Continuing to use Extroverts after an update means you accept the revised terms.
